# Changelog

## 0.1.0-alpha

First playable release. Client-side only, Fabric, Minecraft 26.3.

- One faint recipe is shown in the crafting table, the 2×2 inventory grid, the furnace, the blast furnace, and the smoker.
- Tab places the ingredients for one craft. Shift+Tab places as many as will fit. `[` and `]` cycle the other suggestions.
- Pressing Tab again adds one more item to every slot.
- The suggestion looks at your inventory, what you just picked up, and what is already in the grid. It starts with gear you are missing, a furnace when you have ore, food when you are hungry, then materials.
- An item on your cursor counts. In a furnace, Tab also places fuel: coal, logs, or planks.
- A Tab icon sits under the result. Hovering the faint result shows the usual item tooltip.
